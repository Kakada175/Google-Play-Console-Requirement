SafeMove respects your right to privacy and data protection. In accordance with applicable data privacy laws and Google Play's User Data Policy, you have full authority to request the permanent deletion of your SafeMove account and all associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account using either of the following two methods:

Method 1 Directly Within the SafeMove Mobile App (Instant)

If you have the SafeMove Customer app installed on your device, you can permanently delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the SafeMove app.
  2. Go to Settings or tap your Profile tab.
  3. Select Edit Profile.
  4. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  5. Confirm your selection to permanently erase your account and associated credentials.
Method 2 Submit a Request via Web / Email (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit an account deletion request to our support team:

  • Recipient: support@safemoveexpress.com.kh
  • Subject Line: SafeMove Account Deletion Request
  • Required Information:
    • Your registered mobile phone number (including country code, e.g. +855).
    • Your full name as registered in the app.
Click Here to Send Deletion Email
Important Notice: Once your account deletion request is processed, your account is permanently deactivated and cannot be restored. Any active bookings, loyalty points, or promotional vouchers associated with your account will be forfeited.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon verification and processing of your deletion request, the following information will be permanently removed from our active databases:

  • User Profile: Full name, registered phone number, email address, and profile picture.
  • Authentication Records: Account password hashes, OAuth tokens, and session credentials.
  • Saved Locations: Saved home, work, or favorite delivery and pickup addresses.
  • Device Identifiers: Push notification tokens (FCM) and device registration IDs.
  • Real-time Location Logs: Any transient GPS tracking history linked to your account.

Data Retention Policy

Certain records may be retained in secure archives for a limited period in compliance with statutory obligations:

  • Financial & Tax Compliance: Completed order invoices, payment transaction receipts, and accounting records must be preserved for the minimum period mandated by applicable commercial and taxation laws.
  • Dispute & Fraud Prevention: Minimal transaction logs may be retained solely to resolve ongoing disputes, chargebacks, or to prevent fraudulent misuse of the service.

Retained records are strictly isolated, access-restricted, and never used for marketing or commercial purposes.

Processing Timeline

In-app deletion requests are processed immediately upon confirmation. Email deletion requests are verified and executed within 7 business days. A confirmation email or SMS will be dispatched once the deletion is finalized.
