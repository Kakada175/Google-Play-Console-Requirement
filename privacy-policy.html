SafeMove Express ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our customers and users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, protect, and use your information when you access or use the SafeMove Customer mobile application and related logistics services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality moving, courier, and express delivery services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Details: Your name, mobile phone number, email address, and account login credentials when you register or sign in.
  • Precise & Approximate Location Data: When the SafeMove app is open, we collect your location coordinates to determine pickup and drop-off points, calculate delivery distance and fares, and facilitate live tracking with assigned drivers.
  • Camera & Photos: If you take or upload photos of cargo, items to be moved, or delivery confirmation receipts, the app accesses your camera or gallery with your permission.
  • Device & Log Information: Basic device hardware specifications, OS version, IP address, and system diagnostic logs to prevent fraudulent bookings and maintain app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Create, verify, and manage your SafeMove account.
  • Match your delivery request with the nearest qualified drivers and trucks.
  • Provide live order tracking and push notifications about your delivery status.
  • Calculate accurate delivery pricing and process payments.
  • Provide customer support, resolve inquiries, and improve our services.

3. How Information is Shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data sharing is limited to:

  • Assigned Delivery Drivers: Your name, phone number, and pickup/delivery addresses are shared with the driver assigned to fulfill your booking.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party technology partners:
    • Mapbox / Google Maps Platform: For map rendering, routing, and address geocoding.
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging: For transmitting push notifications regarding booking updates.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Where required by applicable law, regulation, or court process.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide delivery services or fulfill legal obligations.

Account Deletion: You have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and associated personal information at any time directly within the app by navigating to:

Settings > Profile > Edit Profile > Delete Account

You may also contact our support team to request permanent deletion of your profile and data.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard technical measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers, to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
